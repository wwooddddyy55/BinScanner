Color thresholds (HSV, 0-255)
Blob shape filter

Detection only counts the single largest connected red blob in the box (not scattered red pixels), and its bounding-box height/width ratio must fall in this range - rejects thin streaks like glare or a sliver of a passing car.

Infrared (night) detection

When the camera's IR illuminators are on, the image is monochrome and color detection can't work at all - this mode instead compares the box against a stored "bin confirmed absent" reference photo and looks for the largest blob of pixels that changed. Mode is picked automatically per snapshot based on average color saturation.
